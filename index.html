Questions

What is a beat maker?

A beat maker, also written beatmaker, is a tool for making beats. This beat maker app puts drum pads on iPhone and iPad so you can play drums, bass, melody, or a sample.

How do I make a beat?

Pick a sound pack, tap the drum pads, and play a rhythm. To make beats, start with the drums, then add bass and melody from the same pack. A lesson lets you practice one beat and replay it.

What is a drum pad?

A drum pad plays one sound when you tap it: a kick, a snare, a hat, a bass note, or a sample. Beat Maker shows those drum pads in a grid.

Is this an Akai MPC?

No. MPC and Akai are names owned by Akai Professional. Beat Maker is a separate app. If you searched for an MPC, an Akai pad, or a drum pad to make beats, you can play this one free on iPhone and iPad.

Is beat maker the same as beatmaker?

Yes. Beat maker and beatmaker are the same words, and so are beat maker app and beatmaker app. They mean an app for making beats with drum pads.

Is the beat maker app free?

Yes. Beat Maker is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
